Our Story

At KEVI Hair & Beauty, we are more than just a salon; we are a destination for self-care and transformation. Located in the heart of Riverwalk, Gaborone, our mission is to provide a welcoming, relaxing, and uplifting environment where you can unwind and leave feeling your absolute best.

Our team of dedicated professionals stays up-to-date with the latest trends and techniques to ensure you receive top-tier service, whether you're here for a quick trim or a complete makeover.
